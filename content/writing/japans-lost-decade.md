Japan’s experience after its asset-price bubble raises enduring questions about banking, demand, deflation, and the limits of policy responses.

The original paper, **“The Causes of Japan’s Lost Decade and its Effects on Monetary and Fiscal Policy,”** examines that period and its policy implications. It was written on 16 April 2021, as recorded on its title page.
