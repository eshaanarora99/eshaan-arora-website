What happens when an asset-price boom gives way to years of weak growth? Japan's Lost Decade raises lasting questions about banking, deflation, demand, and the limits of monetary and fiscal policy.

In this paper, I examine the causes of Japan's prolonged stagnation and the policy responses that followed. Written in April 2021, it brings together the economic history of the period and the questions it left for policymakers.
